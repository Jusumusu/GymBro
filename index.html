Full-body muscle-building plan

Repeat this same plan 3 days a week (e.g., Monday / Wednesday / Friday) with at least one rest day between sessions. Expect about 55–75 minutes including warm-up and rests.

How to run each workout

1 · Warm up. Do about 5 minutes of easy cardio or movement. Before bench press, do 2–3 lighter warm-up sets; before leg press, do 1–2 lighter warm-up sets.
2 · Work through the exercises in order. Use a comfortable full range of motion, with controlled technique. For bench press use a spotter or safety bars.
3 · Pick challenging weights. Aim to finish most sets with roughly 1–2 good reps left in reserve. Start more conservatively when learning a new lift.
4 · Enter the actual weight and reps for every set. Check Done when that set is finished. For dumbbell lateral raises, record the weight of one dumbbell.
5 · Save the completed workout. Your first saved workout becomes your baseline. On later workouts, follow the suggested rep or weight target, adjusting down if form or recovery suffers.

For muscle growth, aim for enough food and protein (often around 1.6–2.2 g protein/kg body weight/day) and consistent sleep. Stop or modify movements that cause pain.

How automatic progression works

First completed workout: saves your actual weights and reps as your baseline. No previous numbers are assumed.

Next workouts: the target for each exercise comes from your last completed session. Aim to add 1 rep to your lowest-rep set while keeping good form and around 1–2 reps in reserve.

When every set reaches the top of the range at the same weight, the tracker suggests the smallest practical weight increase next time. Default increments are 2.5 kg for most compound lifts, 1 kg per hand for lateral raises, and 1.25 kg for arm cables. Adjust to the actual weight stack increments available. You can edit the increments below.

If your reps drop: it suggests repeating the weight rather than forcing an increase. Suggestions are conservative, never mandatory; reduce the load if technique or recovery suffers.
